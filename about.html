Who We Are

A Taste of Home,
Far From Home

4Bites Café was born from a simple dream: to bring the vibrant, soulful flavors of Nepal and South Asia to the heart of London. Our founders, inspired by the bustling street food stalls of Kathmandu and the warm hospitality of Himalayan culture, set out to create a space where every meal tells a story.

Our open rooftop-style café provides a relaxing environment with bright natural lighting, fresh air, and a welcoming atmosphere that transports you to the terraces of the Himalayas.

We believe food is more than sustenance—it's a bridge between cultures, a celebration of heritage, and an invitation to explore new flavors.
